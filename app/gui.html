Uptime

jolt low-health jolt menu open hidden cut visible cut video position

Game uptime against video time. Uncut footage is a straight diagonal line; a jump up is removed footage, a drop is a pause or relaunch. Scroll to zoom, drag to move, double-click for the whole scan. Click a jolt to jump the video to its frame. Hover between two jolts to see how much game time passed; click to pin it.

Click a row to zoom to it and jump the video to the last jolt before it. Click the status to mark the jump; marks are saved in the project. Jolts is the count of agreeing jolts before and after. Detector shows the largest scene-change score in the gap (8 or more usually means a visible cut, but opening a menu also scores high).
