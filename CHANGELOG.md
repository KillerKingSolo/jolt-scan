# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-02

Why update: first public release.

- Paste a YouTube link or open a video file, trim it, and scan it for hidden cuts with jolt cracking.
- Finds the HUD by itself, including health, low health and the hunger bar under menus.
- Diagonal uptime chart like the Jolt Cracker site, with zoom, gaps, a draggable playhead and a frame-exact video panel.
- Mark each cut as real, not a cut or unsure, and export the results as CSV or HTML.
- The scanner rerolls Java `nextInt(3)` draws that overflow a 32-bit int, the same as Minecraft. A self-test checks this.
- Tick Studio files download and are checked at setup instead of being stored in the repo.
